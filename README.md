# ArabTrail — دليل عملي للعملات الرقمية بالعربية

Practical, up-to-date crypto guides in Modern Standard Arabic — buying USDT, using Binance & OKX, P2P trading, wallets, security, and the basics — with country-specific coverage across the Arab world.

🌐 **Live site: [arabtrail.com](https://arabtrail.com/)**

## What's inside

- **Exchanges** — Binance & OKX guides, fees, KYC, registration (`/exchanges/`)
- **Countries** — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman (`/countries/`)
- **USDT** — buying, transfers, networks (TRC20 / BEP20 / ERC20), fees (`/usdt/`)
- **P2P** — safe buying & selling, choosing sellers, avoiding scams (`/p2p/`)
- **Wallets & security** — wallets, seed phrases, 2FA (`/wallets/`, `/security/`)
- **Basics** — blockchain, Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins (`/basics/`)

## Editorial

Content is fact-checked against official sources and follows a documented [verification methodology](https://arabtrail.com/how-we-test/). Educational content only — not financial, legal, or religious advice.
